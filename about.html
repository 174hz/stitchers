About Stitchers

We believe every talented stitcher deserves to be found

Stitchers was built to connect Canada's incredible community of seamstresses and dressmakers with the clients who need them — directly, fairly, and beautifully.

Our Story

Born from a frustrating search

It started with a bridesmaid dress that needed alterations and no way to find a reliable seamstress nearby. Google returned outdated listings. Facebook groups were hit or miss. Word of mouth only went so far.

Meanwhile, talented seamstresses and dressmakers across Canada were struggling to reach new clients — despite having skills that people desperately needed.

Stitchers bridges that gap. A beautiful, simple platform where skilled textile artisans build their professional presence and clients find exactly who they need — fast.
