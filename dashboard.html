Linda O. Pro Tip
5 hours ago · Calgary
Pro tip for working with silk charmeuse: Always use a microtex needle (size 60/8 or 70/10) and tissue paper underneath when sewing seams. The paper tears away cleanly and prevents the fabric from slipping or snagging on the feed dogs. Game changer for my bridal work! 🪡
